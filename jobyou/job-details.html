Jal Shakti Vibhag Recruitment Apply 2026

by admin963 — June 13, 2026

The Jal Shakti Vibhag has officially released a new recruitment notification for 2026, inviting offline applications for 32 vacancies across various positions, including Multipurpose Worker, Para Pump Operator, and Para Fitter. Eligible and interested candidates can download the application form and submit it before the closing date.

Recruitment Overview

  • Organization: Jal Shakti Vibhag
  • Advertisement Number: JSDA-EA-II-CB-Para Staff/2026-27/2802-05
  • Total Vacancies: 32 Posts
  • Application Mode: Offline
  • Job Type: Part-Time
  • Application Fee: Free (Nil for all categories)

Vacancy & Salary Details

  • Multipurpose Worker: 21 Posts | Rs. 5,500/- Per Month
  • Para Pump Operator: 8 Posts | Rs. 6,600/- Per Month
  • Para Fitter: 3 Posts | Rs. 6,600/- Per Month

Eligibility Criteria

1. Educational Qualification

  • Multipurpose Worker: Must have passed Middle (8th Standard) or its equivalent from a recognized school board.
  • Para Pump Operator: Must have passed Matriculation (10th) along with an ITI certificate in Electrician, Wireman, Diesel Mechanic, Pump Mechanic, Motor Mechanic, or Pump Operator cum Mechanic trade.
  • Para Fitter: Must have passed Matriculation (10th) along with an ITI certificate in Fitter or Plumber trade.

2. Age Limit (As of April 30, 2026)

  • Minimum Age: 18 Years
  • Maximum Age: 45 Years
  • Note: Age relaxation is applicable as per government rules.

Selection Process

The selection of candidates will be determined through the following stages:

  • Shortlisting of applications based on basic qualifications.
  • Skill Test (where applicable for technical trades).
  • Physical Test.
  • Final Merit List preparation.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official department portal to download the detailed notification and print the application form layout.
  2. Fill out the application form carefully with your personal details, academic history, and contact details.
  3. Attach self-attested photocopies of mandatory documents, including your 8th/10th mark sheets, ITI certificates, age proof, and passport-size photographs.
  4. Submit the completed application packet via Registered Post, Speed Post or hand to the official designated address before the final closing date.
